# Vivotiv -- Content & Copy Guidelines

Reference for writing all Vivotiv content. Follow these rules exactly.

## How We Write Copy

1. **Open with the outcome.** What does the reader get? State it plainly. No overpromising.
2. **Explain how.** Be specific and concrete about how we deliver that outcome.
3. **Use real numbers.** "Saved 4 hours/week" not "saves time". Specific fines, specific thresholds, specific timelines.

## What We Avoid

- Feature lists longer than 4 items. Lead with consequences, not specs.
- Self-focused language ("Our scan checks", "We leverage"). Stay on the reader's problem.
- Superlatives ("best", "fastest", "most comprehensive").
- Marketing speak ("Streamline", "Supercharge", "Revolutionize").
- AI-sounding copy (punchy fragment pairs, dramatic reveals, forced rhyming).
- Em dashes.

## Core Principles

- **Sell outcomes, not technology.** Clients don't care about the stack. They care that their problem gets solved.
- **Build from pain.** Every feature and every piece of copy should tie back to a problem with real negative consequences. If it doesn't hurt to ignore, deprioritize it.
- **Sound human.** AI content preference is at 26% among consumers. Real point of view, real specificity, and real tone are the scarce resources. Write like a person who knows this domain, not like a copywriting framework.
- **One niche, one channel, one offer.** Swedish SMBs with aging websites. Free scan as the entry point. Don't dilute.

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Swedish SMBs (1-20 employees) with outdated websites. They know their site is a problem but lack the budget or contacts to fix it. They respond to specific, plain-language explanations of what's wrong and what it costs them.

**Secondary:** Small web agencies (1-5 people) looking for efficiency. 40-80 hours per client migration is their pain point.

## Voice & Tone

- Professional but approachable.
- Direct. No filler, no fluff, no flourish endings.
- Explain technical concepts in plain language.
- Confident but not arrogant.
- Swedish copy should feel natural, not translated from English.

## Market Focus

Sweden first. The regulations we surface (GDPR, ePrivacy, EU Accessibility Act) are EU-wide, but we frame them through the Swedish enforcement angle: IMY, Swedish fines, Swedish legal references. This makes the copy concrete and specific rather than generic. A Swedish business owner knows IMY. "EU data protection authorities" is abstract.

The English site (vivotiv.com) can broaden to EU-wide framing when we expand. For now, one market, one language, one enforcement context.

## The Free Scan as Sales Tool

The scan follows the "diagnose before you prescribe" pattern. Doctors don't sell surgery, they show you the X-ray. The scan is the X-ray.

1. Free scan with no commitment
2. Results create urgency (red/orange scores)
3. Each issue explains how we solve it (not how you solve it)
4. CTA leads to the product

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

~40% of AI-generated answers reference Reddit. To appear in AI-generated recommendations:

- Be mentioned in relevant forums and threads
- Have clear, structured content that AI can parse
- Get listed in directories and review sites
- Zero-click searches are at 62%. Presence in decision surfaces matters more than raw traffic.
