# Vivotiv -- Marketing Context

Reference doc for copywriting and positioning. Use this to inform landing page copy.

## Target Audience

### Primary ICP: Swedish SMBs with aging websites
Small businesses (1-20 employees) running WordPress or other legacy CMS sites that are slow, outdated, and expensive to maintain. They know their site is a problem but lack the budget or developer contacts for a custom rebuild.

What they say: "Vi borde gjort om hemsidan for evigheter sedan men det kostar ju en formogenhet" / "Our website is embarrassing but we can't afford a developer."

### Secondary ICP: Web agencies looking for efficiency
Small agencies (1-5 people) that manually rebuild client sites. 40-80 hours per migration. AI tool that cuts this to hours = margin play.

## Positioning

"Paste your website URL. Get a modern, fast Next.js site you can edit in plain Swedish. No developer needed."

This is NOT a website builder (Squarespace, Wix). NOT a vibe-coding tool (Lovable, Bolt). This is a migration tool -- takes what you already have and makes it modern.

## The Free Scan Angle

The scan exploits a well-known sales pattern: diagnose before you prescribe. Doctors don't sell surgery -- they show you the X-ray. The scan is the X-ray.

**Pitch flow:**
1. Free scan -- no commitment, just paste your URL
2. See the problems -- red/orange scores create urgency
3. Understand the fix -- each issue explains how we solve it
4. Get the solution -- "We rebuild your site with all of this fixed"

**What we DON'T do:**
- Don't scare people with jargon
- Don't show raw Lighthouse JSON
- Explain everything in plain Swedish/English
- Every problem links to "how we fix this" (not "how you fix this")

## EU Compliance as Selling Point

The European Accessibility Act (EAA) became enforceable June 28, 2025. WCAG 2.1 AA is now mandatory for businesses with 10+ employees or 2M+ EUR turnover. IMY (Swedish DPA) has been issuing formal warnings for dark patterns in cookie banners since April 2025. Most Swedish SMBs have no idea they're non-compliant. This creates urgency.

## Landing Page Checklist

- Hero: URL input field prominently centered
- Brief explanation of 6 scan categories
- 3 steps: Paste URL, Enter Email, Get Results
- Video demo later (not for V1)
- Social proof once we have it
- FAQ: "What happens to my email?", "How long does the scan take?", "What do you check?"
- Swedish on vivotiv.se, English on vivotiv.com
- No "subscribe to updates" -- the URL input IS the CTA
- No superlatives, no distracting animations
- Clean, modern, Linear/Vercel aesthetic

## Voice & Tone

- Professional but approachable
- Direct, no fluff
- Explain technical concepts in plain language
- Confident but not arrogant
- Swedish copy should feel natural, not translated from English
